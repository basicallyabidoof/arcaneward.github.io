About Us

"A rare translator who can bridge the gap between the highly technical world and the executive boardroom."

Sean Juroviesky is a leading cybersecurity strategist who brings a decade of front-line experience from the tech, medical, and non-profit sectors to the main stage. As a senior cybersecurity architect for the global music platform SoundCloud, they don't just talk about theory — they live on the front lines of cyber defense every day.

"Transforming security from a technical cost center into a core business enabler."

Sean is a respected speaker at elite cons like DEF CON and Blue Team Con who excels at demystifying complex threats. Their keynotes focus on practical strategy, cultural change, and aligning security with business outcomes.
